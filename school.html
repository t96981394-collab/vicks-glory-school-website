About our school

A school where children feel safe, seen, and supported.

At Vicks Glory Private School, we believe that a strong school experience is built on trust, respect, and genuine care. We are not a giant school trying to impress with excess — we are a close-knit, family-oriented community where every child matters.

With a small student population and a caring atmosphere, we help students grow in confidence, character, and academic discipline. Our goal is to help each learner discover their strengths and become prepared for the future with honesty, dignity, and purpose.
